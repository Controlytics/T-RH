## Still open / known issues

1. **`/api/config/cleaning-profile-assignment` 403 for SUPERVISOR**: the FE requests this endpoint from `mobile-wrapper.tsx` line 161 even though SUPERVISOR doesn't have `CONFIG_READ`. SWR catches the 403 and logs `[SWR Error] Permission denied`. Non-blocking — the FE handles gracefully. Should either grant the perm or skip the request for non-admin roles.

2. **Identifier-map cache lag** (the "Filter not found. Ensure you scanned while online first to cache identifiers." error): even with fresh-sync on app open, there's a window where the IndexedDB identifier-map isn't yet populated. Operator workaround: wait for the "Data Synced" indicator to turn from "Syncing…" → green before scanning. A long-term fix would be to gate the scan-station scan input on `dataCached === true`.

3. **CDP-driven RFID scan simulation**: `Input.dispatchKeyEvent` and `Input.insertText` both produce keystrokes too slow for the FE's 150ms gap-flush detector. Dispatching via `document.dispatchEvent(new KeyboardEvent(...))` inside `Runtime.evaluate` works (5ms inter-key) and IS the right path for future automation. The physical RFID reader is unaffected — only CDP automation hits this.

4. **Race-condition fixes deferred** (operator explicitly chose to keep yesterday's behavior over the race fix): the duplicate-checklist symptom traced to two distinct races —
   - **Read path**: `getCurrentActions(filterId, cachedState.actions)` returns the cached tape blob when non-empty; the blob can be stale because four writers update the same IDB key without sequencing (`submitChecklist` cache rewrite, `appendChecklistCompletion`, SWR `/current-state` refresh, advance handlers).
   - **Write path**: `appendChecklistCompletion`'s `afterStage` was re-read from cache at submit time; concurrent writers could flip `cs.currentState` between dialog open and submit, so the completion logged with the wrong `afterStage` and the executor's gate stayed pending.

   Both fixes are documented in commits `57a8907` and `6b8b8fe` (now reverted). If/when we want to ship them again, they need to be tested with a profile that has multiple consecutive checklists between stages to confirm they don't break the dryer countdown panel path.
